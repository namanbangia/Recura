    01 — The gap

    The patient came once. The clock ran out. Nobody called.

    Aesthetic and wellness medicine is the rare part of healthcare that behaves like retail: cash-pay, elective, high margin, repeatable on a schedule. Toxin fades in three to four months. Filler in nine to twelve. A weight-loss programme bills monthly by design. Every patient is a subscription waiting to be written, and almost nobody writes it.

    The people who could sell, won’t

    Injectors and nurses are clinicians. Asking a patient to add a package mid-treatment is the part of the job they like least, so it quietly does not happen.

    The clinic is shut when the patient decides

    She wants filler at 11pm. There is nobody to sell it to her, and by morning the impulse is gone.

      11 — Objective

      What this pitch is for

      There is a class of clinic — cash-pay, appointment-bound, repeat by nature — whose revenue problem no CRM solves today, because the missing piece is not a record or a pipeline. It is a clock, a wallet, and proof.

      Recura builds that piece, keeps it measurable, and keeps it open: every platform on the board above, plus a custom path for the ones that are not.

      Concept stage. The numbers are modelled, the integrations are designed, and the reason for showing it this early is to shape it with the people whose platforms it would live inside.
